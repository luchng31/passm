Subcommands:
  derive     --password <pw> --salt <hex>          prints master_key + vault_key hex
  encrypt    --in <f> --out <f> --password <pw>    plaintext -> PASSM1 blob (fresh salt)
  decrypt    --in <f> --out <f> --password <pw>    PASSM1 blob -> plaintext
  vault-add  --vault <f> --password <pw> --title <t> --username <u>
             --password-value <p> --url <u> --notes <n>
  vault-list --vault <f> --password <pw>           prints "id title username" lines,
                                                   deleted entries marked [deleted]

THE UNLOCK SEAM (what the app's unlock will do, proven by the CLI):
  parse_header(blob) -> params + salt -> derive_master_key(password, salt, params)
  -> derive_vault_key(master) -> envelope::decrypt(vault_key, blob)

DESIGN NOTE: salt is a per-vault constant, not per-encryption
-------------------------------------------------------------
vault-add re-encrypts with the ORIGINAL header salt + params (only the nonce is
fresh). The vault key is derived from password + header salt, so a fresh salt on
re-encrypt would change the key and the password could no longer unlock the
vault. First implementation used a fresh salt -> vault-list/decrypt after
vault-add failed with AuthenticationFailed; caught by the vault-add tests.
